What is AI in Healthcare?

THE FUTURE

Why Does AI Matter in Medicine?

Traditional drug discovery can take many years. AI can help researchers analyze large amounts of scientific data and identify promising directions for further investigation.

AI does not replace doctors or guarantee successful treatments. Instead, it can provide useful tools to support medical research and healthcare decisions.
